Behind the pixels

Dubai has been my creative playground for more than two decades.

For 15+ years, the event industry is where many of my biggest ideas have come to life. I turn ambitious concepts into memorable brand experiences — ideas that attract attention, tell a story, transform spaces, and give audiences something worth remembering.

Throughout my career I have collaborated with some of the region’s most recognized brands and organizations, including Richard Mille Polo, Al Fursan Endurance Cup, Aramco, Ithra, Amazon, the Ministry of Transport and Logistics, Jordan Tourism, Emirates Red Crescent, Dubai Cares, Fatafeat, Discovery Channel, and Al Qadsiah.

My world covers brand identities, event concepts, key visuals, experiential design, campaigns, social and digital, creative presentations, outdoor advertising, print production, visualization, creative direction, websites, and studio management.

From the first rough sketch to the final production file, I combine creative thinking with hands-on experience. Along the way I’ve mastered ambitious briefs, impossible deadlines, last-minute changes — and the famous request for “just one tiny edit.”

Great design should do more than look good. It should make people stop, feel, remember, and respond.
